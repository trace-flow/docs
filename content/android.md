The Android app is built natively, with the conventions of Android, and uses the same account, the same data and the same rules as the web app.

## What you can do

- **Today and Home.** Your tasks for the day and the dashboard of what needs attention.
- **Requirements, tasks and risks.** Browse, read, create and update them, with their status and links.
- **Sprints.** See what is in a sprint and how it is going.
- **Tests and releases.** Test management, releases and traceability, on the plans that include them.
- **Documents and activity.** Open what a requirement points at and see what changed.
- **Notifications.** Updates on the work you follow.
- **Reports.** Health and coverage on the plans that include them.

## Same rules

A feature a plan does not include is shown as a Pro feature, as on the web. What a role may not do is hidden in the app, and the data layer enforces it as well.

## Live data

The app always shows what is in your workspace right now. Nothing it has stored on the phone is treated as the truth.

## Signing in

The same email and password as the web app.
