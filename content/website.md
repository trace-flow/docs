The website is where people learn about TraceFlow before they sign in. It is separate from the workspace itself.

## What is on it

- **Features.** The modules, grouped as Plan, Execute, Verify and Track.
- **Solutions.** How the same graph helps embedded, IoT, automotive, medical, aerospace, robotics and industrial teams.
- **The digital thread.** The idea that ties it together: every requirement connected to the work, tests and release that follow from it.
- **Pricing.** Free, Pro and Enterprise.
- **Roadmap and documentation.**
- **Contact.** How to reach sales and support.

## Interactive tour

A visitor can open the tour without signing up. They get a private sandbox of the web app filled with sample data, to click through at their own pace. Nothing they do reaches anyone else, and it is cleared away afterwards.

## Where it fits

The website links into the [web app](web.html) for sign-up and sign-in.
