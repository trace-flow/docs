## What you can do

<div class="cards chip-teal">
<div class="card" data-icon="grid"><b>Start with Home</b><p>Your own tasks, the active sprint and what needs a look. Project leads see the team's view.</p></div>
<div class="card" data-icon="folder"><b>Work in projects</b><p>Requirements, tasks, bugs, sprints, releases, risks and documents, by project and module.</p></div>
<div class="card" data-icon="flask"><b>Verify</b><p>Test plans, cycles, cases and runs, with each result shown the same way everywhere.</p></div>
<div class="card" data-icon="link"><b>Trace</b><p>Follow a requirement to its tasks, tests, risks and commits, as a matrix or a mind map.</p></div>
<div class="card" data-icon="chart"><b>Report</b><p>Health, coverage and velocity across a project.</p></div>
<div class="card" data-icon="users"><b>Manage the organisation</b><p>People, roles, invitations, project settings and the plan in use.</p></div>
</div>
