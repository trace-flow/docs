The web app is the full TraceFlow workspace. It talks only to the [API](api.html), so it always shows what is in the system rather than a copy of it.

## What you can do

- **Start with Home.** A dashboard of your own tasks, the active sprint, and what needs a look. Project leads see the team's view.
- **Work in projects.** Requirements, tasks, bugs and to-dos, sprints, releases, risks and documents, grouped by project and module.
- **Verify.** Test plans, cycles, cases and runs, with the status of each result shown the same way everywhere.
- **Trace.** Follow any requirement to its tasks, tests, risks and commits, as a matrix or a mind map.
- **Report.** Health, coverage and velocity across a project.
- **Manage the organisation.** People, roles, invitations, project settings and the plan in use.

## Signing in

Email and password. A new person signs up with a six-digit code sent to their address, then chooses a password. People invited to an organisation follow the link in the invitation.

## Look and feel

Warm paper and ink, with colour kept for meaning: green-teal for finished work, amber for work in motion, coral for blocked work. Light and dark themes, chosen in preferences.

## Public pages

Pricing, terms and privacy are public. The interactive tour opens a sandbox filled with sample data, with no sign-up.
